Halo’s kernels halve a GLM-5.3-Flash LoRA step

General kernels · Halo’s kernels ·

One LoRA step of GLM-5.3-Flash at EP8 and 8,192 tokens on B300, parts sorted by their time on Halo’s kernels. General kernels: torch’s attention kernel and upstream’s hyper-connections. Each part is the mean of the ranks over forward, recompute and backward. The per-part clock adds about 2.6 s over an unclocked step.
