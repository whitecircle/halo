A schematic of two layers in a row, Y = X @ W1 then Z = f(Y) @ W2, such as the two projections of an MLP. Every rectangle is drawn in proportion to its shape, with N twice K. Both ranks hold the same X, because the ranks of one tensor-parallel group share one batch. Each rank computes its slice of Y from the whole X without communication and applies f to it. The next operation needs the whole Z, so it waits for the all-reduce.
